# Honolulu Affordable Housing Policy Evaluation (ROH Chapter 32)

**Author:** Rafael Munoz with Gemini AI Assistance
**Role:** Graduate Research Assistant, UHERO (University of Hawai'i Economic Research Organization)

## Executive Summary
This repository contains the end-to-end data pipeline, geospatial analysis, and automated web scrapers used to evaluate the economic feasibility and bureaucratic processing efficiency of the City and County of Honolulu's ROH Chapter 32 (Bill 7) affordable housing program.

The pipeline proves whether the policy's statutory rent caps (100% Area Median Income) impose a genuine financial penalty on developers or function as a density loophole by comparing statutory limits against localized open-market rents.

## Setup & Prerequisites
To replicate this pipeline, ensure the following environments and dependencies are installed.

**Python 3.10+ (Scraping & Aggregation)**
* `selenium`, `webdriver-manager` (Chrome WebDriver is automatically handled)
* `pandas`

**R 4.2+ (Spatial & Econometric Analysis)**
* `tidyverse`, `lubridate`, `scales` (Data wrangling and visualization)
* `sf` (Geospatial buffer operations and projections)
* `showtext` (Custom typography rendering)

## Execution Pipeline
The pipeline must be executed sequentially to ensure data continuity:

1. `python scripts/1_dpp_metrics_plan_reviews_scraper.py`
   * Initializes headless Chrome to bypass legacy City/County UI and extract real-time application processing milestones and timeline data.
2. `python scripts/2_dpp_csv_compiler.py`
   * Aggregates the individual application CSVs generated by the scraper into structured MASTER datasets.
3. `Rscript scripts/3_roh_ch32_spatial_analysis.R`
   * Ingests the master files, projects coordinates to UTM Zone 4N, executes 0.5-mile localized neighborhood buffering, and conducts the core comparative affordability analysis.

## Outputs
Execution of the pipeline generates institutional-grade deliverables into the `/outputs/` directory.
